Travel Destination Page

  1. Kerala Backwaters

    2. Description :

    Explore a peaceful network of interconnected canals, rivers, and lakes in Kerala. Visitors can enjoy traditional houseboat rides, lush green landscapes, and a glimpse into local village life

    DURATION COST
    Short Cruises (1 to 3 Hours) ₹875 to ₹2,500 per person

  2. Goa

    3. Description :

    A stunning coastal destination famous for its beautiful beaches, vibrant nightlife, and Portuguese-influenced architecture. Relax by the water, explore historic churches, or savor delicious seafood.

    DURATION COST
    3 to 5 days Budget/Hostels: ₹500 to ₹2,000

  3. Varanasi

    4. Description :

    Known as one of the oldest inhabited cities in the world, Varanasi is a major cultural and spiritual center in India. Experience the evening Aarti ceremony, the ancient temples, and the sacred River Ganges.

    DURATION COST
    2 to 3 days Budget: ₹1,500/day (Hostel dorms, street food, local tuk-tuks).

  4. The Golden Temple

    5. Description :

    Located in Amritsar, this is the holiest shrine in Sikhism. Visitors are drawn to the stunning golden architecture, the serene environment, and the Langar—a community kitchen that serves free meals to everyone.

    DURATION COST
    1 to 3 Hours Free
